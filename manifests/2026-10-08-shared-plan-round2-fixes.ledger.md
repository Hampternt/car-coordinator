## Decisions and deviations

- 3: of the three options, the stopped tab keeps following the room (sending, receiving) and Backups is the floor. Rejected: the live tab merging another tab's v1 write (diff against the stored base). It cannot tell edits the room has not seen from a stale plan that lacks the room's newer edits, and a wrong guess sends an undo of the other manager's confirmed edits; it also resends edits a live writer is sending itself. A stopped tab sending is as safe as a second PC: it builds on its own replica; only its writes to this browser's storage were ever the danger, and those stay off until reload. The Backups copy is one entry, replaced while it is the newest (`Store.snapshot` gained `opts.replace`), so typing in a stopped tab never pushes older backups out.
- 5: Create's seq-0 snapshot leaves the same hole, so it gets the op too (the brief named compaction and Push; "every snapshot" covers Create). A browser that merely joins a room with no op past its snapshot sends none: that would change the round-2 check "neither sent anything just for joining", and the hole there closes with the first edit.
- 5, a round-2 check changed by design: Push's "and the push sent no op of its own" (exactly one op in the room) became "the push sent no change of its own: one op with none, after its snapshot".
